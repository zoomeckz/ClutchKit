ClutchKit 3.5 - stream toolkit for TikTok LIVE Studio, OBS Studio and Twitch
============================================================================
Counters for anything you want to track (deaths, wins, kills, attempts...), each with
its own hotkeys, shown live on stream with your own font, colors and animations.
Widgets: goal bars, a subathon timer, top gifter / liker lists, viewer counts and one
alerts overlay for TikTok and Twitch.
Actions: gifts, follows, shares, joins, likes and chat commands play sound alerts,
press keys in your game, change your counters or control OBS.


INSTALL
-------
1. Unzip this folder and double-click ClutchKit.exe.

2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (Windows shows this for small apps that aren't code-signed yet.)

3. Click "Install". ClutchKit opens and adds itself to the Start menu
   (and the desktop, if you leave that box ticked). No admin rights needed.

   Prefer not to install? Click "Run without installing" - ClutchKit then
   keeps its files next to ClutchKit.exe.


ADD IT TO TIKTOK LIVE STUDIO
----------------------------
In ClutchKit, open "Display":

  Link overlay (recommended, see-through):
    1. Click "Copy LIVE Studio link".
    2. In LIVE Studio: Add source > Link > paste (Ctrl+V) > Add source.
    3. Resize and place it. You only do this once - changes you make in
       ClutchKit show up on stream automatically, and it doesn't matter
       whether you start LIVE Studio or ClutchKit first.

  Capture window:
    Pick "Capture window", then in LIVE Studio: Add source > Window capture >
    "ClutchKit - Overlay". Keep that window open (it can sit behind your
    game, just don't minimize it).


TWITCH
------
On the "Twitch" page, type your channel name and click "Connect" (no login needed).
ClutchKit then reads your Twitch chat, bits, subs and raids (for chat commands and the
alerts overlay) and your Twitch viewer count. It never writes in your chat. Follows
need a Twitch login, so they are not included.


USING OBS STUDIO INSTEAD
------------------------
Open the "OBS" page in ClutchKit.

  Counters and widgets:
    1. Pick what you want (Counters, a goal, the subathon timer or a top list)
       and click "Copy OBS link".
    2. In OBS: Sources > + > Browser > OK, paste the link into URL, set the
       suggested width and height, click OK. Do this once per overlay.

  Let actions control OBS (OBS 28 or newer):
    1. In OBS: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings > tick "Enable WebSocket server".
       Click "Show Connect Info" to see the port and password.
    2. In ClutchKit, enter the port and password (address 127.0.0.1 when OBS runs
       on this PC) and click "Connect". Tick "Connect automatically" so it connects
       by itself whenever OBS is open.
    3. On the "Actions" page pick "Control OBS" (see ACTIONS below).

  To stream to TikTok from OBS you need a TikTok stream key; not every account has
  one. Gifts and other LIVE events still come through the TikTok LIVE page below.


USING IT
--------
The first counter is "Deaths" with these keys:
  Numpad +   add one
  Numpad -   remove one
  Numpad *   reset to 0

On the "Counters" page you can rename it, add more counters (up to 8) and give
each one its own keys: click a key button, then press the key you want.
The keys keep working while you're in a game, and your game still gets the key press.

Closing the window keeps ClutchKit running by the clock (red icon with three lines).
Double-click the icon to open it again, right-click it to exit.

LANGUAGE: ClutchKit starts in your Windows language (English, Español, Português,
Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Tiếng Việt). Change it on the "General" page.


WIDGETS (GOALS, SUBATHON TIMER, TOP LISTS, VIEWER COUNTS, ALERTS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
On the "Widgets" page. Each widget is its own Link source, so you can put it anywhere:
click its "Copy link", then in LIVE Studio: Add source > Link > paste. "Preview" shows it
in your web browser. Widgets use the font and colors of your first counter.
  Goals         a bar for likes, follows, shares, coins, gifts, viewer joins or one of
                your counters. "raise by" moves a reached goal up (e.g. +1000).
  Subathon      press Start. Gifts (seconds per coin), follows, shares and likes add
                time. +5 min / -5 min, Pause, Reset. If ClutchKit closes while it runs,
                it waits paused until you press Go on.
  Top lists     top gifters (coins) and top likers, for this LIVE or all time.
  Viewer counts TikTok, Twitch or both added together, with your own text before and
                after the number (e.g. "Twitch:" 23). Add as many as you like; each has
                its own link, or use the "all in one" link to show them one under the other.
  Alerts        gifts, follows, shares (TikTok) and subs, gifted subs, bits, raids
                (Twitch) pop up one after another. "Test alert" sends two test pop-ups.
This LIVE's top lists and goals start over by themselves when your next LIVE starts
(or click "New LIVE: start over"). Goals, timer and top lists need the TikTok LIVE
connection (see below), except goals that show a counter.


ACTIONS (SOUND ALERTS, KEYS IN YOUR GAME)
-----------------------------------------
1. Get a free Euler Stream API key: on the "TikTok LIVE" page click
   "Get a free key", make an account and copy your API key.
   (TikTok has no official way to read LIVE events; Euler Stream does it for you.)
2. On the "TikTok LIVE" page, type your TikTok username, paste the key and
   click "Connect". Tick "Connect automatically" so you never have to do it again.
   If you aren't live yet, ClutchKit waits and connects when you go live.
3. On the "Actions" page, click "+ Add" on a topic bar. First pick what triggers it:
     Gift          the gift's name, e.g. Rose (several: Rose, GG). Empty = any gift.
                   min. coins: only gifts worth at least this much (e.g. 100 for big gifts).
     Follow, Share, Viewer joins
     Likes         every N likes (e.g. every 1000).
     Chat command  a word viewers type, e.g. !boom (several: !boom, !bomb).
                   per viewer (s): each viewer can use it once every this many seconds.
                   ClutchKit only reads the chat; it never writes in it.
   Then what it does:
     Play a sound  pick an MP3 or WAV file (it's copied into ClutchKit's "sounds"
                   folder) and set the volume. Sounds play one after another.
     Press a key   the key to press in your game, and how long to hold it.
     Type text     types text, e.g. a Minecraft command. "First" = a key pressed
                   before typing (T opens the chat in Minecraft).
     Change a counter  add, subtract, set to, or add the gift's coins
                   (e.g. every Rose = Deaths +1, or a coin counter).
     Control OBS   switch scene, show source, hide source, mute, unmute - "for (s)"
                   puts it back after that many seconds (0 = it stays). "in scene" is
                   where the source is; leave it on "(the scene on stream)" if unsure.
                   Also: save replay (the replay buffer must be running), start or
                   stop recording. Runs once per trigger (a x10 combo = once).
   And how often:
     Times         how many times each time it's triggered.
     cooldown (s)  don't run it again within this many seconds
                   (joins start at 10 s, so a busy room can't spam sounds).
     Every gift in a combo: a x10 Rose combo presses the key 10 times
                   (untick: once per combo; sounds always play once per combo).
4. Use "Test" (or "Test with a fake event"), click into your game and watch.

Keeping lots of actions tidy:
- Topics are boxes for your actions, e.g. one per game ("Minecraft", "Sounds").
  "+ New topic" makes one; each topic can be folded up, renamed, deleted, or
  switched off as a whole (its actions then don't run).
- Each action folds to one line - click the arrow or its name to open it.
  "Open all" / "Close all" fold everything at once.
- Search finds actions by gift, sound, text or topic name.
- "Duplicate" copies an action right below the original; "Topic" moves it.
- Profile: "Save..." remembers which topics are on (e.g. one per game); pick a
  profile to switch to it.
- "Export..." saves topics with their sounds in a .ckpack file to share or keep.
  "Import..." adds someone's pack; imported topics start off, so check them first.

Sound alerts play through your PC's speakers/headset, like any other sound.
In LIVE Studio, make sure your desktop/system audio is turned on so viewers hear them.

Tips:
- "Only press keys in": pick your game, so gifts never type into chat or Discord.
- Set an "On/off key" to stop actions instantly, e.g. while you're in a menu.
- "Stop and clear" stops the sound that's playing and empties the queue.
- If your game runs as administrator, Windows blocks keys from normal programs.
  ClutchKit tells you, and "Restart as administrator" fixes it.
- Games with strict anti-cheat (e.g. Valorant, Fortnite) may ignore pressed keys.


SOMETHING NOT WORKING?
----------------------
Open the "Debug" page:
1. Tick "Detailed logging (debug mode)".
2. Make the problem happen (e.g. connect and send a gift, press the key, play the sound).
3. Click "Save debug report". It's saved in ClutchKit's "debug" folder and copied, so you
   can paste it straight into a bug report (link at the bottom of the page).
"Check layout in all languages" looks for text that is cut off and saves a screenshot of
every page in every language (debug\layout).
If ClutchKit ever crashes, a crash report is saved in the same folder.
The report never contains your Euler Stream key, but it does contain the activity log,
which has viewer names from your LIVE. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it.


UNINSTALL
---------
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > ClutchKit > Uninstall.


PRIVACY
-------
The LIVE Studio overlay talks to ClutchKit on your own PC only (127.0.0.1).
The keyboard is only used to react to your chosen hotkeys; nothing you type is stored.
The only internet connection is the TikTok LIVE connection, and only when you
connect: your TikTok username and Euler Stream key go to Euler Stream, which
sends back your LIVE's gifts. The key is saved in settings.ini on your PC.
The OBS connection (only if you turn it on) goes to the OBS address you enter, normally
this PC. Its password is saved in settings.ini too.
The Twitch connection (only if you turn it on) reads your channel's public chat from
Twitch, and asks decapi.me (a free service) for your viewer count - only the channel
name is sent.

SETTINGS WINDOW: drag its edge or maximize it and the text grows or shrinks to fit.
Settings > "Scale text with the window size" and "Smooth scrolling" turn these off.
The top lists keep viewer names and their coins/likes in leaderboard.txt and live.txt
on your PC only ("Clear" on the Widgets page empties them).
